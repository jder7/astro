# Special Ptolemaic Aspect Configurations

Overview of the primary multi-planet patterns built only from the five Ptolemaic aspects (0°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 180°). Each entry lists planets involved, the geometric shape, typical orbs, and how to construct the figure (no interpretations).

## Stellium
- **Planets**: 3+  
- **Geometry**: Tight cluster, usually within one sign or ~30°, so most planets are mutually conjunct.  
- **Orb guide**: Often within ~5–10° between neighbors across the cluster.  
- **Construction**: A bundle of overlapping conjunctions; all planets occupy the same sector.

## T-Square
- **Planets**: 3 (sometimes more)  
- **Geometry**: One opposition capped by two squares (A↔B opposition, C square to both).  
- **Orb guide**: Squares/oppositions typically ±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: The “focal” planet is the one forming both squares.

## Grand Trine
- **Planets**: 3  
- **Geometry**: Three 120° links forming an equilateral triangle.  
- **Orb guide**: Trines usually ±6–8° (often ~±7°).  
- **Construction**: A–B, B–C, and C–A all 120° apart.

## Kite
- **Planets**: 4  
- **Geometry**: A Grand Trine with a fourth planet opposing one triangle point and sextile the other two (kite “spine” plus two sextile sides).  
- **Orb guide**: Trines ~±6–8°; opposition ~±8–10°; sextiles ~±5–6°.  
- **Construction**: Grand Trine A–B–C, add D opposite A; D sextile B and D sextile C.

## Grand Cross (Grand Square)
- **Planets**: 4  
- **Geometry**: Four points every 90°; two oppositions plus four squares.  
- **Orb guide**: Squares/oppositions typically ±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: A↔C and B↔D in opposition; each point square to its neighbors.

## Grand Sextile (Star of David)
- **Planets**: 6  
- **Geometry**: Hexagram—alternating 60° and 120°; two interlaced Grand Trines linked by sextiles.  
- **Orb guide**: Sextiles ±5–6°; trines ±6–8°.  
- **Construction**: Two equilateral triangles; each vertex sextile to two vertices of the other triangle (6 trines + 6 sextiles).

## Mystic Rectangle
- **Planets**: 4  
- **Geometry**: Two oppositions stitched by two trines and two sextiles into a rectangle.  
- **Orb guide**: Oppositions ±8–10°; trines 6–8°; sextiles 5–6°.  
- **Construction**: A↔C and B↔D oppositions; A sextile D & trine B, C sextile B & trine D.

## Trapeze (Cradle)
- **Planets**: 4  
- **Geometry**: Three sextiles in a row (A–B–C–D) with an opposition across the ends (A↔D), forming a cradle/trapezoid.  
- **Orb guide**: Sextiles ~±5–6°; opposition ~±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: A sextile B, B sextile C, C sextile D, plus A opposite D.

### Orb ranges (general)
Ptolemaic aspects commonly use roughly ±5–10° depending on aspect and speed of the bodies: sextiles ≈±5–6°, trines ≈±6–8°, squares/oppositions ≈±8–10°. Each pattern requires all constituent aspects to sit within their respective orbs to count.
