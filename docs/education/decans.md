<a id="purpose"></a>
## Purpose

Decans refine a sign into **three “faces”** — three movements inside the same 30° field.

They help when:
- a sign is **over-emphasized** (stellium / chart ruler / angles),
- a transit is **tight** (you want the *phase* of the transit),
- two people share the same sign but live it differently (synastry nuance).

<a id="what-a-decan-is"></a>
## What a decan is

Each sign spans **30°** and is divided into three parts:

- **1st decan:** 0°–9°59′
- **2nd decan:** 10°–19°59′
- **3rd decan:** 20°–29°59′

In the source material used for this project, each decan is described by:
1) a **subruler** (planetary tone),
2) a **keyword cluster** (psychological quality),
3) an **auxiliary constellation** (mythic background note).

> Note: the 10° decans are stable, but your house system and zodiac (tropical/sidereal) affect which degrees land where.

<a id="how-to-use-decans"></a>
## How to use decans

### Natal
- Read the sign as the **main frequency**.
- Use the decan to refine the expression:
 - **1st decan:** raw archetype / primary lesson
 - **2nd decan:** training method / skill acquisition
 - **3rd decan:** integration / outcome / mastery test

### Transits
- A planet moving through a sign typically unfolds in **three phases**.
- With slow planets, decan shifts can mark clear changes in theme.

### Synastry
- Same-sign placements feel different if the decans differ — the subruler changes the flavor.

**How to read the three faces (developmental):**

- **1st decan:** establishes the sign’s core lesson in lived form.
- **2nd decan:** trains the skill/mind needed to carry that lesson with competence.
- **3rd decan:** tests integration — can the sign express its gift without distortion?

<a id="decans-as-a-triadic-model"></a>
## Decans as a triadic model

The three decans can be read through two complementary lenses:

- **Matter / Consciousness / Motion** — the emphasis in manifestation
- **Physical / Emotional / Mental** — the personality envelope carrying the emphasis

**Definitions:**
- **Physical (etheric):** embodiment, habits, conditions, resources and the instrument of daily life 
- **Emotional (astral):** desire nature, feeling-tone, attachment/aversion and relational meaning 
- **Mental:** meaning-making, strategy, integration and deliberate direction 

### Mapping

| Decan | Span | Aspect emphasis | Envelope emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st decan | 0°–9°59′ | **Matter** | **Physical** |
| 2nd decan | 10°–19°59′ | **Consciousness** | **Emotional** |
| 3rd decan | 20°–29°59′ | **Motion** | **Mental** |

### Use

For any placement (planet in a sign):

1) **Sign** describes the archetypal tone 
2) **Decan** shows where the tone is carried most strongly: **Matter/Physical**, **Consciousness/Emotional** or **Motion/Mental** 
3) **Subruler** refines the decan with a planetary accent (and its ray note, where relevant)

#### Behavioural synopsis by decan

- **Decan 1 — Matter / Physical:** Aries ignites through routines, constraints and tangible conditions. Subruler: **Jupiter**; keywords: *expansion, absorption*.
- **Decan 2 — Consciousness / Emotional:** Aries stakes through desire, sensitivity and relational meaning. Subruler: **Sun**; keywords: *rulership, identification*.
- **Decan 3 — Motion / Mental:** Aries drives through strategy, timing and deliberate direction. Subruler: **Mars**; keywords: *conflict, fight*.

#### Integrated example

Example: **Mars in Aries** — a sprint-style launch where initiative matters more than polish. In Decan 1 the expression lands first in Matter/Physical, visible through tempo, body-led initiative, decisive starts (subruler Jupiter; expansion, absorption). The 2nd decan shifts the weight to Consciousness/Emotional, colouring the same impulse with heat, pride, courage tests (subruler Sun; rulership, identification). By Decan 3 Motion/Mental dominates and the theme is directed through tactics, selection of battles, directed force (subruler Mars; conflict, fight).

**Auxiliary constellation notes (mythic sub-tones):**

- **Decan 1 — Cetus:** Cetus is the monster of the unconscious which engulfs consciousness. Thus it symbolizes the possibility that incipient consciousness is engulfed anew by the sea of the subconscious, of monstrous dimensions.
- **Decan 2 — Cassiopeia:** Cassiopeia is the enthroned queen, matter reigning with total control and in excess. In the myth there is no punishment for her hubris, save the necessity of solving the problems she creates.
- **Decan 3 — Perseus:** Perseus, the awaited one, is the hero who saves. This he does by brandishing Medusa’s head, which is a symbol of the power of reflection and meditation.

**Auxiliary constellation notes (mythic sub-tones):**

- **Decan 1 — Capricornus Sagitta:** Sagitta is the arrow killing the eagle which torments Prometheus. All the work of Hercules in Capricorn has to do with the final liberation of the human soul from the obsessive, straining and fixating effects of the negative patterns of the subconscious (Pluto, Hades).
- **Decan 2 — Delphinus:** The Greek word delphos means womb, the capacity to generate, build and give shape and adelphos means brother, son of the same womb. Apollo appeared in Delphi disguised as a dolphin, after having killed the original dragon of the oracle, Delphine or Python, claiming the oracle for himself.
- **Decan 3 — Aquila:** Aquila, the Eagle, is the Solar Angel, whose sovereignty, independence, sharp vision and elevated position enable the initiate to progress all the way to the final liberation (of both). Altair (the Bird) is the principal star of this constellation, a real bird. As always, the wings point to the devic (angelic) forces of the fourth cosmic ether.
