## Orientation

A special configuration is an **energy circuit**:

- **Planets** are the *operators* (what functions are acting).
- **Signs** are the *frequency bands* (how those functions behave).
- **Aspects** are the *wiring* (how the functions must cooperate or contend).
- **Developmental octave** (psychological / esoteric lens): the *same* circuit can run as habit (reactive), skill (integrated) or contribution (collective).

<a id="how-to-read-any-pattern-4-layer-decode"></a>
### How to read any pattern (4-layer decode)

1) **Geometry** → what kind of circuit is it (pressure engine, talent loop, polarity bridge, adjustment funnel)?
2) **Roles** → identify: apex/focal, base, release point, missing leg, “spine.”
3) **Frequency** → add element + mode + sign tone (what makes this circuit feel like *this*).
4) **Octave** → ask: is this showing as compulsion, competence, or stewardship?

<a id="orbs"></a>
### Orbs

A pattern counts only if **every constituent aspect** is within orb. Typical working ranges (adjust to your method and planet speeds):

- **Conjunction**: ±6–10°
- **Sextile**: ±4–6°
- **Square**: ±6–10°
- **Trine**: ±6–8°
- **Opposition**: ±6–10°
- **Quincunx (Yod)**: often tighter, ~±2–3° (many astrologers keep it strict)

<a id="stellium"></a>
### Stellium

- **ID**: `stellium`
- **Planets**: 3+ planets  
- **Aspects**: conjunction  
- **Aspects label**: Conjunctions  
- **Geometry**: Clustered within ~30° (often one sign) with overlapping 0° links.  
- **Orb**: Planets within ~5–10° of each other across the cluster.  
- **Construction**: Conjunction-series bundle occupying one tight sector.  
- **Description**: Soul pod or karmic bundle concentrated in one slice of the chart; a chorus of voices singing one theme.

**Circuit mechanics (how the parts interact)**
- Conjunctions **merge** functions: planets don’t “negotiate,” they *cohabitate*. The strongest planet (by dignity, angularity, or rulership) tends to “set the tone,” with the others becoming modifiers.
- A stellium often behaves like a **single organ** in the psyche: high capacity, but also high sensitivity.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: over-identification (“I am this theme”), tunnel vision, compulsive emphasis, blind spots elsewhere.
- **Integrated**: specialization becomes craft; the cluster turns into a reliable competence (skill + maturity).
- **Collective**: the stellium becomes an instrument others can lean on, a stable channel of contribution.

**Integration & healing**
- Balance by consciously developing the **opposite sign** / missing axis qualities (range training).
- Avoid “all-in” living; build recovery cycles (sleep, quiet, movement) so the cluster doesn’t burn the whole system.

**Other**
- Many modern sources describe a stellium as an **unusually strong focus** in a sign or house and a place where life feels “turned up.”

<a id="t-square"></a>
### T-Square

- **ID**: `t_square`
- **Planets**: 3 planets  
- **Aspects**: opposition, square  
- **Aspects label**: Opposition + Squares  
- **Geometry**: Opposition capped by two 90° squares, forming a T spine.  
- **Orb**: Squares/opposition typically ±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: A ↔ B opposition with C square to both (C = focal).  
- **Description**: Cosmic lever that presses toward the focal planet; tension seeking breakthrough and mastery.

**Circuit mechanics**
- The opposition creates a **polarity** you tend to externalize (projection) until you can hold both truths.
- The focal planet is the **pressure node**: it receives two squares, so it becomes the “decision point” where energy must become skill.
- The “missing leg” (opposite the focal) acts like a **release valve**: those sign/house qualities stabilize the whole figure.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: reactivity, blame loops, “either/or” thinking; focal overwhelm.
- **Integrated**: the focal becomes trained leadership, you learn sequencing, boundaries, and self-regulation.
- **Collective**: you become a synthesizer under pressure, someone who can hold tension and produce solutions.

**Integration & healing**
- Name the two gifts of the opposition (both valid, incomplete alone).
- Give the focal planet a discipline (reps, structure, accountability).
- Practice the missing-leg quality daily in small acts.

**Other**
- Some describe T-squares as indicating **conflict** but also **dynamic motivating energy**.

<a id="grand-trine"></a>
### Grand Trine

- **ID**: `grand_trine`
- **Planets**: 3 planets  
- **Aspects**: trine  
- **Aspects label**: Trines  
- **Geometry**: Three 120° links in an equilateral triangle.  
- **Orb**: Trines usually ±6–8° (often ~±7°).  
- **Construction**: A–B–C all 120° apart forming a closed triangle.  
- **Description**: Elemental circuit where energy flows effortlessly; gifts and talents that ask to be awakened consciously.

**Circuit mechanics**
- Trines create **easy circulation**: the planets reinforce each other without friction.
- Because it’s closed, the trine can become a **self-contained comfort loop** unless deliberately aimed.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: complacency, coasting, “I’m fine” while avoiding growth edges.
- **Integrated**: stable inner resource, the trine becomes your platform and recovery center.
- **Collective**: effortless competence becomes **reliable giving** (you use ease to support others).

**Integration & healing**
- Choose one “square-style” practice: deadlines, training, public output, or mentorship, to activate the gift.

**Other**
- Some describe grand trines as **free-flowing, cooperative interaction and natural ability** with the planets involved.

<a id="yod-finger-of-god"></a>
### Yod (Finger of God)

- **ID**: `yod`
- **Planets**: 3 planets  
- **Aspects**: sextile, quincunx  
- **Aspects label**: Sextile + Quincunxes  
- **Geometry**: Two 150° quincunxes converging on an apex, with the base planets sextile.  
- **Orb**: Quincunx typically ±2–3°; sextile ±4–6°.  
- **Construction**: Apex point quincunx to both base planets, which are sextile to each other.  
- **Description**: A fated finger pointing toward a singular mission; awkward adjustments birthing a precise calling.

**Circuit mechanics**
- The sextile at the base is **potential** (opportunity), but it needs choice and effort to open.
- The quincunxes create **non-native fit**: two functions that can’t easily “see” each other must continuously adjust.
- The apex becomes the **pressure of adaptation**: the psyche repeatedly re-tunes until a very specific capacity emerges.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: chronic unease (“something is off”), over-correcting, feeling pulled in incompatible directions.
- **Integrated**: flexibility and honest self-observation; you become skilled at micro-adjustments without losing your center.
- **Collective**: a precise calling develops, a “custom-made” contribution that emerges from persistent refinement (not linear ambition).

**Integration & healing**
- Treat the Yod like a **tuning fork**: adjust the *behavior*, not the identity.
- Keep the quincunx orb strict: if the pattern is real, it’s usually *felt* strongly and acts consistently.
- Watch transits to the apex: they often time turning points.

**Other**
- Astrodienst’s Yod article emphasizes that Yods can **change the way you read a chart**, often describing a path with bends and side-roads rather than a straight line.
- Astrodienst’s Astrowiki defines the Yod structurally: two quincunxes to an apex, base sextile.- Other commentator notes that Yod can bring **irritation/frustration**, requiring flexibility and open-mindedness.

<a id="kite"></a>
### Kite

- **ID**: `kite`
- **Planets**: 4 planets  
- **Aspects**: trine, opposition, sextile  
- **Aspects label**: Grand Trine + opposition + sextiles  
- **Geometry**: Grand Trine with a fourth point opposing one trine point and sextile to the other two.  
- **Orb**: Trines ~±6–8°, opposition ~±8–10°, sextiles ~±5–6°.  
- **Construction**: A–B–C trines, with D opposite A and sextile B and C (kite spine and sides).  
- **Description**: A grand trine given a tailwind; latent grace made directional through a compelling axis.

**Circuit mechanics**
- Grand Trine = stable talent field.
- Opposition = **vector** (direction) and “wake-up call.”
- Sextiles = practical pathways that translate talent into outcome.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: swing between comfort (trines) and drama/avoidance (opposition).
- **Integrated**: purpose emerges; talent is aimed with discipline.
- **Collective**: “gift + mission” geometry, contribution that’s both natural and compelling.

**Integration & healing**
- Identify the “spine” (the opposition): let it define your direction.
- Use sextiles as weekly behavioral bridges (small consistent output).

**Other**
- Some describes the Kite as combining **grand trine natural ability** with the **striving tension** of an opposition.

<a id="grand-cross-grand-square"></a>
### Grand Cross (Grand Square)

- **ID**: `grand_cross`
- **Planets**: 4 planets  
- **Aspects**: opposition, square  
- **Aspects label**: Oppositions + Squares  
- **Geometry**: Four points every 90°: two oppositions plus four squares.  
- **Orb**: Squares/oppositions typically ±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: A↔C and B↔D oppositions; each is square to its neighbors.  
- **Description**: Four-way crossroads of will; crucible of tests that forges resilience and integration of polarities.

**Circuit mechanics**
- Two oppositions = two simultaneous polarity lessons.
- Squares = constant demand for refinement in timing, self-control, and reality contact.
- The cross tends to produce **high output** when you stop resisting the training.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: chronic stress, control strategies, burnout cycles.
- **Integrated**: rhythm mastery, you learn sequencing, prioritization, and boundaries.
- **Collective**: structural capacity, you can hold complexity and make it workable for others.

**Integration & healing**
- Build a “rule of life”: sleep, movement, order, commitments that match your actual capacity.
- Integrate one axis at a time; don’t try to solve the whole cross in one heroic push.

**Other**
- Some describes the Grand Cross as known for **powerful driving energy**.

<a id="grand-sextile-star-of-david"></a>
### Grand Sextile (Star of David)

- **ID**: `grand_sextile`
- **Planets**: 6 planets  
- **Aspects**: sextile, trine  
- **Aspects label**: Sextiles + Trines  
- **Geometry**: Hexagram/Star of David: alternating 60° and 120° points.  
- **Orb**: Sextiles ±5–6°; trines ±6–8°.  
- **Construction**: Two interlaced Grand Trines linked by six sextiles.  
- **Description**: Star-of-David resonance; a merkaba-like lattice inviting flow between worlds and effortless synergy.

**Circuit mechanics**
- Trines provide **coherence**; sextiles provide **open doors** that require intention.
- Without conscious activation, the pattern can remain latent (lots of potential, not always lots of output).

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: diffusion (“I could do anything”), scattered activation, procrastination by possibility.
- **Integrated**: selection and orchestration, you become a curator of your own power.
- **Collective**: network-building and synthesis: connecting people, skills, and systems across domains.

**Integration & healing**
- Pick one sextile bridge and make it behavioral (a weekly practice linking two planets’ functions).
- Commit to one meaningful track for a season; don’t live in “options.”

**Other**
- Some note that a true Grand Sextile with bodies in orb is **very rare** and describes the six-point spacing around the zodiac.

<a id="mystic-rectangle"></a>
### Mystic Rectangle

- **ID**: `mystic_rectangle`
- **Planets**: 4 planets  
- **Aspects**: opposition, trine, sextile  
- **Aspects label**: Oppositions, Trines, Sextiles  
- **Geometry**: Two oppositions stitched by trines and sextiles into a rectangle.  
- **Orb**: Oppositions ±8–10°; trines 6–8°; sextiles 5–6°.  
- **Construction**: A↔C and B↔D; A sextile D & trine B, C sextile B & trine D.  
- **Description**: An envelope of grace containing polarity; tension held within a harmonious frame for inspired service.

**Circuit mechanics**
- Oppositions provide the **tension** (the “why change is needed”).
- Trines and sextiles provide the **release points** (how the tension becomes constructive instead of destructive).

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: avoidance (“I’ll stay in the easy parts”) or quiet inner conflict.
- **Integrated**: self-regulation; you can hold opposing needs without collapsing into extremes.
- **Collective**: mediation talent, “balancer” energy that can stabilize groups.

**Integration & healing**
- When tension spikes, don’t decide immediately: regulate with a trine resource first, then act via a sextile step.

**Other**
- Cafe Astrology describes the Mystic Rectangle as two oppositions connected by **trines and sextiles**, with those harmonious aspects acting as **release points** for the oppositions’ tension, and notes a tendency to put talents to good use.

<a id="trapeze--cradle"></a>
### Trapeze / Cradle

- **ID**: `trapeze`
- **Planets**: 4 planets  
- **Aspects**: opposition, sextile  
- **Aspects label**: Opposition + Sextiles  
- **Geometry**: Three sextiles in a row with an opposition across the open ends.  
- **Orb**: Sextiles ±5–6°; opposition ±8–10°.  
- **Construction**: A sextile B sextile C sextile D, with A↔D in opposition.  
- **Description**: A rocking cradle that can soothe or stir; supportive links framing a singular axis to resolve.

**Circuit mechanics**
- The sextile chain creates a **learning sequence** (A→B→C→D): you build resolution by stages.
- The end opposition creates the “main question” your life keeps returning to.

**Developmental octave**
- **Reactive**: stuck in comparison (the two ends), comfort through “middle steps” that delay the real decision.
- **Integrated**: incremental alchemy, you stop demanding instant resolution and build it.
- **Collective**: teaching pattern, you learn by linking, then you naturally show others how to link.

**Integration & healing**
- Treat it like a curriculum: define the smallest behavior that links each sextile pair, repeat until automatic.
- When triggered, work the chain; don’t jump straight to the ends.

**Other**
- Cafe Astrology (citing J. Negus) suggests the Trapeze is a generally harmonious configuration that requires balance and may indicate timely “aid from the outside.”
- Some also use “Cradle” as an aspect pattern name, highlighting creativity and resilience in conflict (terminology varies by author).

<a id="how-patterns-interact"></a>
## How patterns interact

Patterns often stack. When they do, read them in this order:

1) **Tightest orb figure** (strongest circuit).
2) Any figure involving **Sun/Moon/Angles** (identity + regulation).
3) Any figure anchored by **Saturn/Pluto** (structure and deep transformation).

Common interactions:

- **Stellium inside a T-Square**: the “chorus” becomes the focal pressure engine, specialization is forced to mature fast.
- **Grand Trine + T-Square**: ease meets friction; you oscillate between comfort and crisis until you build consistent output.
- **Mystic Rectangle + Stellium**: strong focus, but the rectangle provides balancing pathways, a good “talent-to-service” setup.
- **Yod touching a bigger pattern** (apex or base planet also part of a cross/trine): the Yod acts like a fine-tuning mechanism that makes the larger pattern feel “fated” or unusually precise.
